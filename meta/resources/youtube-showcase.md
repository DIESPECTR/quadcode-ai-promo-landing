# Showcase sources and review limits

## Review scope

These are discovery counts, not a claim that every video was watched. The channel's displayed total can differ from tab counts. No full-channel footage/transcript audit was completed.

Themes found: 3D applications, architecture, music tools, kitchen configurators, chess, fantasy games, trading/dashboard apps, AI video and promotional Shorts. For the landing, prioritize variety and evidence over adding every video. Architecture/model comparisons are omitted to keep the story focused.

## Why BLOOM CARE is the mini-case
The guide provides an explicit reproducible brief and sequence, stronger evidence than video titles alone:
1. Claude Sonnet: brand/product/audience plus Japanese minimalism, cream/blush palette, serif headlines, whitespace.
2. Request hero, bestsellers, serum spotlight, formulation story and collection; agent supplies layout and copy.
3. Add subtle scroll/hover polish and inspect the recorded page.
The guide's 12-second scroll-through is a recording length, not build time. No credit cost is known. The case does not claim a working checkout. Additional official evidence: https://guides.quadcode.ai/ui_views/assets/evidence/beauty-brand-landing/step-1.webp and https://guides.quadcode.ai/ui_views/assets/video-bloomcare-landing.mp4.

UGC guide: GPT-Image actor, GPT-Image product compositing, Kling motion. Original video: https://guides.quadcode.ai/ui_views/assets/video-ugc-ads-workflow.mp4. These are suggested workflow models, not guaranteed economics or advertising outcomes.
